Field notes

Three things to notice.

01 / PITCH

Frequency

Higher frequency generally means a higher perceived pitch for a pure tone.

02 / LOUDNESS

Amplitude

Increasing amplitude increases signal strength; perceived loudness also depends on frequency and listening conditions.

Wave shape

Different wave shapes contain different mixtures of harmonics.
