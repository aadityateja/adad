QUARK WORLD

Protons and neutrons are hadrons. They contain valence quarks bound by the strong interaction, mediated by gluons.

u = UP QUARK
d = DOWN QUARK

Proton valence content uud

Two up quarks and one down quark.

Neutron valence content udd

One up quark and two down quarks.
