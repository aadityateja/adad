SCALE 10⁻¹⁰ METRES / 002

The atomic
realm.

Ordinary matter is built from atoms. An atom has a compact nucleus surrounded by electrons described by quantum states. Its size is overwhelmingly empty space in the classical picture, but the quantum reality is richer than tiny orbiting planets.

The nuclear
boundary.

The nucleus occupies a tiny region at the centre of the atom. It contains positively charged protons and electrically neutral neutrons. Nearly all an atom's mass resides here.

QUANTUM CHROMODYNAMICS / 004

Inside the
nucleon.

Protons and neutrons are composite particles. Their structure involves quarks, gluons and a dynamic sea of quantum fields. Quarks carry colour charge; gluons mediate the strong interaction.

u
u
d

A proton has valence-quark content uud. A neutron has udd. Gluons and sea quarks also contribute to their internal dynamics. Isolated quarks are not observed: they remain confined within colour-neutral composite states.

CHEMICAL STRUCTURE / 006

The elements.

The periodic table organises chemical elements by atomic number. Each element has a nucleus with a particular number of protons. Electrons determine much of its chemical behaviour.
